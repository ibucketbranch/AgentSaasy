ChiefOS - Document Intelligence, Local-First

The swarm, observed.

Eight specialized AI Agents file, deduplicate, and read deadlines out of thousands of documents on one always-on machine. This page replays one orchestrator cycle, event by event: every dispatch, every model call, every routing decision. The badge on the canvas states exactly what data you are watching.

01

One Cycle, Replayed

The orchestrator wakes, reads what arrived, and dispatches the specialists. Work routes up a tier ladder: deterministic rules first, then a fast local classifier, then a local analyst model, and only behind a budget gate the cloud. Uncertain documents go to a human review queue instead of a wrong guess.

02

Who Is Who

Each agent does one job and hands off. Nothing in the engine can delete a file.

ORCHESTRATORWakes hourly, decides what needs doing, dispatches the others.
FILING CLERKAtomic, logged moves into canonical folders.
LOST & FOUNDSecond opinion on files nothing else could place.
DEDUP HUNTERFinds duplicates by content fingerprint, never acts alone.
PROFILERKnowledge graph of the people and organizations in the documents.
STUDENTLearns new routing rules from every human correction.
AUDITORReviews the system's own code every cycle.
KEEPERKeeps everything running, restarts what falls over.
