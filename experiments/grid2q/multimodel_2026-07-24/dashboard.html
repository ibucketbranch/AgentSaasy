What this run taught us

The CHIL-005 trap is the best discriminator in the exam

An urgent-sounding field note on an asset whose health score (52) sits just above the critical threshold. Every tier fell for it: the frontier once, haiku three times, both locals three times. Boundary judgment under emotional distractors is where model quality separates, not raw recall.

Failures are consistent, not random

Each model failed the same way all three times: llama always omitted the count, always fabricated the same "$86,400 to $0" claim; qwen always computed the same wrong $58,500. At temperature 0 these are stable capability boundaries, which is exactly what makes rubric-gated routing plausible: a query class that fails once fails predictably.

Size does not order the failures

The 3B llama aced the three-step math the 7B qwen flunked three times, while qwen aced the retrieval and synthesis llama flunked. Capability is per-class, not per-size: you route by query class, not by a single model ranking.

The cheap-API story survives; the local story needs Phase 1 proper

gpt-5-nano at 83% remains the efficiency king on counted cells. The locals' 40% and 20% are NOT a verdict on quantization: these are small models judged against a frontier-calibrated rubric, and quantized at that. Phase 1's real question stays open: compare a quantized model against its own full-precision parent to isolate what compression alone costs.
