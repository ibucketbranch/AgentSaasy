Findings
Five things the data shows
1
Same tool choice across all three
All architectures made 1 call to query_assets. Tool selection was not the differentiator — prompt design was.
2
Prompt length is the primary cost driver
48 → 122 → 271 system-prompt tokens with no improvement in answer quality.
3
Output constraints compound the savings
The 150-token output cap produced 53 output tokens vs 70 and 108. Output is billed too.
4
Prompt overhead ratio exposes hidden waste
2.0% → 4.7% → 9.2%. Enterprise pays 9¢ of every $1 just to carry governance boilerplate.
5
The waste is architectural, not AI
GPT-4o-mini produced equivalent business answers regardless of prompt bloat. The model didn't cause the inefficiency.
↗
At Scale: 500K queries/month
Tutorial architecture costs $712 more per year than Optimized — from prompt design choices alone.
Thesis
AEQ = Business Value Delivered / Tokens Consumed
Same model and query, different prompt design → 7–54% cost difference for the same outcome.
Prompt design and output constraints matter even when tool selection is correct.
