What the refresh established

The trap outlived the model generation it was built against

CHIL-005 was designed against gpt-5.2 and now beats gpt-5.6-sol 3 for 3, while the cheaper luna resisted it once. Narrative-over-threshold judgment error is not a small-model or old-model quirk; it's a standing frontier failure mode your instrument detects on demand. This is the white paper's sharpest exhibit.

Deterministic failures survived a full model refresh

With a new frontier reference and partially new judging, qwen produced the digit-for-digit identical fabricated Q5 triple and the identical Q2 near-miss as yesterday, and llama repeated its "$86,400 to $0" fabrication. Failure fingerprints belong to the (model, query class) pair, not to the measurement setup. Certification transfers.

The efficiency story strengthened, with citable prices

Luna matched or beat sol everywhere except nothing (13 vs 12 overall) at 5.1x lower measured cost, and every dollar figure traces to a same-day official price. The old runs' "cheap tier passes" claim now has a reproducible, priceable successor, and the cost delta sits right at the pre-registered 5x GREEN threshold.

Haiku's failure mode is attribution, not reasoning

Re-judged with a working cross-family judge, haiku got orderings and derivations right but repeatedly attached true numbers to the wrong entities (the $46,800 overdue figure applied to a list including non-overdue assets). A distinct failure family the rubric's no-fabrication criteria catch cleanly.

Prior runs (2026-07-23/24, deprecated-model generation), kept for the record

Calibration runs 1-2 (gpt-5.2 era): rubric v1.0 saturated; v1.1 passed on merit (frontier 14/15, nano 5/15 failed). Judge self-contradictions eliminated by the fail-confirmation protocol.
